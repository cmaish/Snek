    How to play

    • 👆
      Steer by dragging your finger anywhere (or point with the mouse, or use the arrow keys).
    • 🍬
      Eat the glowing dots to grow. ⭐ stars are worth extra!
    • 🔢
      Every snake has a number badge. Green = smaller, you can eat it. Red = bigger, run away!
    • 😋
      Bump a smaller snake to chomp it. Crash into a bigger one and you just bounce off — but big snakes can bite your tail! Eat several quickly for a combo.
    • ⚡
      Hold BOOST (or a second finger) to zoom. It costs a little size.
    • 🎁
      Power-ups: 🧲 magnet · ⚡ zoom · ⭐ super star · 2× double · ❄️ freeze · 🍰 candy rain · 🍄 giant
    • 🐶
      Spend coins on new bodies and heads — mix and match! — and on boosters like an Extra Life ❤️.
    • 👑
      Be the biggest snake when time runs out to win!
